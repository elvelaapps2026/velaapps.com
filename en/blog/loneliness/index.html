Loneliness: what the Bible says

Even if your father and mother forsake you, God will not. He promised to be with you always.

Deuteronomy 31:8

8And the Lord, he it is that doth go before thee; he will be with thee, he will not fail thee, neither forsake thee: fear not, neither be dismayed.

Hebrews 13:5

5Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.

Matthew 28:20

20Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

Psalm 27:10

10When my father and my mother forsake me, then the Lord will take me up.
